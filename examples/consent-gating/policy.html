Local example storage

This example has no analytics provider. Its optional sample service stores only the value "running" in this site's localStorage under letemcookie-sample-service. Necessary only or withdrawal removes it. No sample data is sent anywhere. The value otherwise remains until withdrawal or browser storage is cleared.

The consent choice is stored under letemcookie-gating-demo for up to 180 days, or until the example version changes or storage is cleared. This is a product demonstration, not a production cookie policy.
