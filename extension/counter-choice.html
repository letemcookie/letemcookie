A cookie census.

Help the homepage count cookies eaten.

For each cookie banner we see disappear after accepting, the extension sends one counter ping to our Cloudflare server, with no website address or cookie contents.

The server sees your IP address and uses a daily secret-keyed hash to estimate participating connections and limit spam; our code does not store your raw IP, and the hashes expire within two days.

Only the total goes on the homepage. You can turn counting off in extension Settings. Banners get eaten either way.
