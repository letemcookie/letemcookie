Let 'Em Cookie.

Never see a cookie banner again.

Get the extension

Free. Open source. Chrome, for now.

"I hereby accept all cookies, forever. No need to ask me ever again."

After the first banner, the extension hands you this. Copy it, post it, or don't.

Shipping a site? Take the library.

letemcookie.js is a small banner with Accept all and Necessary only. It records the choice locally and stays invisible to anyone running the extension.

<script src="https://letemcookie.com/letemcookie.js" async></script>

Some banners get through. Accept-all is the point. This is not a privacy tool.

This site sets no cookies. It simply accepts yours.
