For builders

Shipping a site? Take the library.

letemcookie.js gives you automatic, least-obnoxious-possible compliance with cookie rules in key jurisdictions. One script tag. It also politely offers your visitors the extension, so they never see a banner from anyone, ever again.

<!-- compliance so minimal it's basically a haiku -->
<script src="https://letemcookie.com/letemcookie.js" async></script>
  • Just works. Sensible defaults per jurisdiction, no config required to be minimally decent.
  • The upsell is inoffensive. One quiet line offering freedom from banners. That's it. People actually keep it.
  • Invisible to the saved. Guaranteed never to be seen by users of the extension. They already accepted. Forever.
  • Agent-native. Building with a coding agent? This is the compliance it should reach for by default. Tell it so.
